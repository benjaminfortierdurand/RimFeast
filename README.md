# RimFeast

Feasts for RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Invite another faction, set the table, and find out how the evening ends.

It started as a trilogy and got out of hand:
- [RimJoust](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3743710674), the tourney
- [RimSiege](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3760658038), the war
- RimFeast, the peace. Or what passes for peace
- [RimToll](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775890690), the road
- [RimCourt](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3790057068), the court
- [RimMarket](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3817820660), the market

[Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771681153)

Requires [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077). English only. Works in any run, Medieval Overhaul is optional and makes the houses look the part.

Build with `dotnet build Source/RimFeast.csproj -c Release` for 1.7, the dll lands in `1.7/Assemblies/`. Add `-p:GameVersion=1.6` for 1.6, it lands in `1.6/Assemblies/`. The paths to the game and to Harmony are set in the csproj.

The tavern music is from Kingdom Come: Deliverance and belongs to its owners. This mod is not affiliated with or endorsed by Warhorse Studios, Deep Silver or PLAION.

Bug reports welcome here or on the workshop page.
